Privacy Policy

How Umrah360 handles personal information and connected-service data.

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are. Umrah360 is a lead-management and customer-engagement platform operated by Amaavigo Digital Solutions (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy applies to our website, application, CRM, integrations, and related services (“Services”).

1. Information we handle

Depending on features used, we may process:

  • Account/workspace data: names, business contact details, authentication identifiers, roles, and settings.
  • CRM and lead data: names, email addresses, phone numbers, company and location details, lead status, assignments, notes, follow-ups, and activity history entered or imported by users.
  • Communications: email and WhatsApp messages and metadata, delivery information, call notes, and conversation history from channels users connect or record.
  • Google data: when you connect Google Calendar or another Google service, account identifiers and the specific calendar/event information permitted by the scopes you approve and needed for the feature you enable.
  • Technical data: browser/device details, timestamps, diagnostic logs, security events, and usage information needed to operate and protect the Services.
  • AI inputs: text or records submitted to enabled AI features to generate summaries, classifications, or suggested replies.

2. How we use information

We use data to provide and maintain the Services; manage leads, contacts, campaigns, communications and follow-ups; enable scheduling; generate user-requested AI outputs; support users; prevent fraud and abuse; troubleshoot errors; maintain security; and comply with law.

3. Google API data

When you connect Google Calendar or another Google service, access is limited to the permissions you approve and the feature you choose. Calendar information may be used to check availability, create or update events, and coordinate meetings. Future Google integrations will be limited to their disclosed features and granted permissions.

  • Google user data is used to provide or improve the user-facing feature you requested.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or transfer it for those purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.
  • Human access is limited to cases necessary to provide the service, with your consent or instruction, for security/support, or where required by law.
  • You can revoke access through your Google Account permissions or disconnect the integration where available.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The actual scopes are shown on Google's consent screen.

4. Meta and other integrations

If you connect Meta or another messaging platform, data may be processed to route messages, maintain conversation history, respond to customer requests, and report delivery status for the feature enabled. Data access depends on permissions and settings. We do not sell platform data.

5. AI and service providers

We may use providers for hosting, databases, authentication, AI processing, email delivery, messaging, and diagnostics. They receive information as reasonably necessary to provide the feature, protect the Services, or comply with law. AI outputs can be inaccurate and should be reviewed before consequential use.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls and provider security measures. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information as needed to provide the Services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on data type, settings, providers, and law.

7. Sharing

We may share information with service providers, third-party services you connect, authorised users in your workspace, a successor in a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards, or authorities where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security. We do not sell personal information.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion of personal information. Workspace users may need to contact their administrator. Third-party permissions can be revoked through the relevant provider's account settings.

9. Data deletion

To request account or personal-data deletion, email sales@umrah360.in with subject “Privacy and data deletion request”. Identify the account/workspace and data concerned; never send passwords or tokens. We may verify your identity and authority. We will respond within a reasonable period and delete or anonymise information where required, except data retained for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or legitimate recordkeeping purposes. Third-party copies may need to be deleted with that provider.

10. International processing and children

We and our providers may process information outside your country, with appropriate safeguards where required. The Services are business tools and are not intended for children to use independently. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our Services or legal requirements change; the new version and date will be posted here. Contact sales@umrah360.in or visit umrah360.in for privacy questions.

Before platform verification, confirm this policy matches your deployed implementation, actual OAuth scopes, service providers, and deletion/retention procedures. This page is not legal advice.
